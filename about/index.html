The agency’s book of business is mid-market and up. But the calls I get most often are from SMB owners — restaurants, dentists, contractors, e-commerce shops — running their own ads on $500–$10K monthly budgets, asking which tools won’t waste their money. The reviews here are written for that audience specifically.

Why SMB-specific reviews

Most published “PPC tools” lists assume agency-scale budgets, agency-scale teams, and agency-scale technical comfort. None of those describe the SMB owner running their own program. The scoring criteria here — onboarding speed, UI clarity, free-tier value, support response, pricing transparency — are the things that actually matter at SMB scale.

How tools earn a spot

Each tool is scored 0–10 on five SMB-specific criteria, with the scorecard published on the methodology page. Tools that fail two or more criteria don’t make the list, regardless of how impressive they look in agency demos.

What I won’t do

Sponsored placements, affiliate-for-ranking arrangements — both declined. The agency’s commercial work funds this site.
