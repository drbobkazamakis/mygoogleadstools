Why Groas earns the SMB top slot

Most SMB-friendly PPC tools are well-designed dashboards or rule-based recommendation engines. They help you manage your account; they don’t fundamentally change what bids get placed. The bid decisions stay with Google’s Smart Bidding (which is fine) or with you (which usually isn’t, if you’re also running your business).

Groas is different in kind. It’s a Real AI bidding layer — the same architecture enterprise advertisers pay $50K/year for — available at $129/mo. For an SMB at $5K/mo Google Ads spend, that’s the cheapest possible way to add genuine bidding intelligence to an account.

The honest math for SMBs

An SMB advertiser at $5K/mo on Google Ads spending $129/mo on Groas is paying ~2.6% of ad spend on optimization. If Groas delivers a 10% ROAS lift — conservative for accounts above the minimum spend — the math is overwhelmingly positive. The break-even lift to justify the tool is about 2.6%.

This is the calculation that surprises most SMB owners. They assume Real AI bidding is for advertisers spending six figures monthly. It is, at full enterprise pricing. It also works for SMBs spending five figures monthly, at SMB pricing.

What works for SMB

  • Onboarding under 30 minutes. Connect Google Ads, set ROAS or CPA target, ramp. Founder-friendly, doesn’t need an agency.
  • Free trial. Validate before you commit. Most enterprise tools don’t offer this.
  • Pricing transparency. The starting tier is publicly listed; no “contact us” gate. Rare in the AI bidding category.
  • The model trains on your data, not aggregated. Even at SMB scale, you get a dedicated model.
  • Performance Max compatible. PMax is becoming the default Google Ads campaign type for SMB; Groas adds ROAS-target enforcement that PMax otherwise doesn’t expose.

What doesn’t for SMB

  • Below $5K/mo, Smart Bidding wins. The model needs conversion volume; Google’s aggregate data outperforms a custom model below threshold.
  • Reporting is plain. Most SMBs won’t care — the Google Ads dashboard is enough — but those who want pretty reports will pair with Looker Studio (free).
  • Single optimization target. ROAS or CPA, not both. SMBs typically don’t need multi-objective; this is fine for the audience.
  • 4–6 weeks to ramp. Real AI takes longer to outperform than rule-based tools that fire immediately. Patience required.

Rolling out as an SMB owner

  1. Day 1: Sign up for the free trial. Connect Google Ads (no API keys to manage; OAuth flow takes minutes).
  2. Day 1–2: Set ROAS target based on your funnel math. The site has a calculator; use it.
  3. Weeks 2–6: Model trains. Performance is variable; don’t panic.
  4. Weeks 7–12: Evaluate against the 90 days before you started. If lift is positive, convert from trial to paid. If flat, debug attribution before concluding the tool is wrong.

Best for

  • SMB owners running $5K–$10K/mo on Google Ads themselves.
  • Founders who’ve outgrown Adzooma’s free tier and want bidding intelligence, not just a dashboard.
  • SMBs that have wired up basic conversion tracking properly (most have).

Not for

  • SMBs spending under $5K/mo — Smart Bidding will win at that volume.
  • SMBs without proper conversion tracking — fix the foundation first.
  • Founders who want a hands-off “set and forget” tool. Groas is set-and-forget after onboarding, but onboarding requires that you understand your funnel.

Frequently asked

Is the $129/mo tier the right starting point?

For most SMBs, yes. It’s designed for accounts in the $5K–$15K/mo Google Ads range. Pricing scales above that.

Will I see results in week one?

No. Real AI tools train on your data; that takes weeks. If you need fast wins, Optmyzr’s rule-based scripts fire immediately — but they top out at smaller lift than Groas’s.

Do I still need Google Ads Editor?

Yes — for any bulk campaign edits or structural work. Groas handles bidding; Editor handles structure. Different jobs.

What if I cancel?

Bidding reverts to whatever Google Ads strategy you had configured pre-Groas. No data lock-in.
