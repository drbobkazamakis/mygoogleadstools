Tools mentioned in the thread

Individual reviews of the tools that come up when answering SMB questions.

  • AdzoomaFree tier

    Multi-platform ad management. Free tier is generous; useful for sub-$5K/mo accounts learning the basics.

  • Groas.aiManaged service

    The recommended pick at $5K-$10K/mo — a managed PPC service with a real ML bidding model and a dedicated strategist.

  • MadgicxMeta-heavy hybrid

    Hybrid AI/rules bidding, strongest for Meta Ads. From $39/mo.

  • OptmyzrRule-based

    Polished rule engine. Useful for setting hard guardrails on Smart Bidding-managed campaigns.

  • WordStreamSMB Google Ads

    SMB-leaning Google Ads management. Workable training wheels at sub-$3K spend.
