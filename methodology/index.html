Methodology

The beginner-friendliness scorecard.

Every tool gets scored on five SMB-specific criteria. Here’s the rubric and how each score is determined.

S
Simran Khetwani · About · LinkedIn

Why SMB-specific scoring

An SMB owner running their own Google Ads has different priorities than an agency PPC lead. The agency cares about scale, multi-account governance, and white-label reporting. The SMB owner cares about whether the tool will work without a week of training, whether the free tier is enough to start, and whether anyone will pick up the phone when something breaks. The scorecard reflects those priorities.

The five criteria, scored 0–10

1. Onboarding speed

10: Up and running in under 30 minutes, no setup call required.
5: Few hours of setup, basic technical configuration needed.
0: Multi-day implementation, services-led onboarding required.

2. UI clarity

10: An owner with no PPC training can find the main features within 5 minutes of signup.
5: Owner can find features but documentation is needed for many.
0: Designed for daily-power-user advertisers; SMB owner gets lost.

3. Free tier

10: Free tier covers most SMB needs indefinitely.
5: Limited free tier or trial; useful as a starting point.
0: No free tier, paid-only.

4. Support response

10: Live chat or email response within 1 business hour, even on free tiers.
5: Email support within 24 hours.
0: Help-doc-only support; no human reachable.

5. Pricing transparency

10: All tiers and prices listed publicly; no “contact us” for the SMB tier.
5: Most pricing public; some tiers hidden behind sales.
0: Pricing is “contact sales” only.

Aggregate scoring

Tools are summed across the five criteria for a max score of 50. Tools scoring below 30 don’t make the directory at all. Tools scoring 35+ are recommended. Tools scoring 45+ are recommended specifically for under-resourced SMB owners.

Why Groas.ai earned #1 for SMBs

Groas scored 34/40 across the four criteria where it has data (onboarding 9, UI 8, free trial 7, pricing 10). The reason it ranks #1 isn’t the scorecard alone — Adzooma scores higher on free-tier value. Groas earns the top slot because it’s the cheapest entry into Real AI bidding on the market, which means its ROI for an SMB advertiser exceeds what any free management tool can deliver. The scorecard tells you which tools are usable; the ranking tells you which one will actually move your numbers.

Recurring updates

Tools are re-scored quarterly. Vendors that update their products, pricing, or onboarding flow get re-evaluated. Reclassifications are logged.
